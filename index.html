Common questions

Where do my new photos go?
“Save as new copies” creates a new folder inside an Export folder in the folder you opened, and shows it as a tab above your photos. Your original photos stay as they are. You can choose another location under Save to.
Can I undo a change?
Yes. Press ⌘Z. When you change photos in place (“Apply in this folder”), the old versions go to your Trash, and Undo brings them back. As long as they’re still in the Trash, you can also restore them from there.
Why does Lightstack ask me to “Allow” access to a folder?
When you open single photos, macOS lets Lightstack see only those files. Lightstack saves its work next to your photos, so it asks once for their folder. Click Allow in the window that opens.
Can I move a crop after saving it?
Yes. Crop the photo again and drag the frame: Lightstack keeps the full photo, so a crop can be adjusted or removed at any time.
Can I look at or change one photo at a time?
Yes. Press Space (or double-click a photo) to see it big, and move with the arrow keys. Turn on any step on the left and press Return to save that photo and go to the next; press C to crop it by hand.
How do I find photos?
Press ⌘F and type part of a name, or a detail such as the camera, a date or a keyword. Press 1–5 to give a photo stars, and use the star button at the bottom to show only photos with that many.
Which photos does Lightstack work with?
JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP and BMP. It saves JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF and PDF.
What does Lightstack need?
A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
