Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

In short: Lightstack works entirely on your Mac. It collects no data, has no internet connection, and your photos never leave your Mac.

No data collected

Lightstack doesn’t collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Lightstack has no network access, so it can’t send anything anywhere.

Your photos

Lightstack only opens the photos and folders you choose. It saves its results in folders you can see, next to your photos or in a place you pick. When you change photos in place, the old versions go to your Trash, so you can restore them.

New photos keep the details stored in the original, such as the date taken, the camera and where it was taken. You choose which of these to keep or remove, and you can write in a date, rating, author, copyright, title, caption or keywords. These details stay inside your photo files.

Search and ratings

Search looks through file names and the details inside your photos while Lightstack is open; nothing is indexed, saved or sent anywhere. Star ratings you give are written into the photo files themselves, like the other details.

Face detection

To center crops on faces, Lightstack finds where a face is in each photo, using Apple’s Vision framework on your Mac. It doesn’t identify who anyone is, and nothing about faces is saved or sent anywhere.

Settings

Your settings, saved presets and recent folders are kept on your Mac only.

Children

Lightstack is often used for school photos. Because it collects nothing, no information about children (or anyone else) reaches us or anyone else through the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
